Support Center

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about our snacks, delivery, and services.

How do I place an order?

Simply navigate to our Store, select your favorite snacks, and click "Order Now". You can checkout securely using Paystack for instant confirmation.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver across major areas in Lagos. Delivery fees vary based on your Local Government Area (LGA) and will be calculated during checkout.

Can I order custom cakes?

Yes! For custom wedding or birthday cakes, please use the "Book Consultation" button on our home page or contact us directly via phone.

Is my payment secure?

Absolutely. We use Paystack for all transactions. Your card details are never stored on our servers and are handled with industry-standard encryption.

What is your return policy?

Since our snacks are fresh and perishable, we cannot accept returns. However, if there is a quality issue, please contact us within 2 hours of delivery.

Do you offer bulk orders?

Yes! If you need more than 100 items for an event, check the Store for the "Order More Than 100" button or email us for a custom quote.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to assist you with any special requests or concerns.
